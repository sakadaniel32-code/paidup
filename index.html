Questions

Things people ask.

Do my clients need to download anything?

No. They get a link, and it opens in their browser like any web page.

How do my clients pay me?

For naira, the Pay button copies your account number so they can transfer from their bank app. For foreign clients, it opens your own Paystack, Flutterwave, PayPal or Stripe link. The money goes straight to you. Setul never holds it.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Setul is built for phones first, and you'll be able to add it to your home screen like an app.

How much will it cost?

We'll share pricing before launch. People on the waitlist hear first.

Who can see my clients and invoices?

Only you. A client sees only the one quote, invoice or receipt you send them.
